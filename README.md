# Neural-Architecture-Search-NAS-Quantization-Knowledge-Distillation-KD-
Wi-Fi Gesture Recognition with Quantization and Knowledge Distillation

This paper proposes a lightweight transformer model for Wi-Fi-based gesture recognition through a combined approach that leverages neural architecture search (NAS), quantization, and knowledge distillation (KD). A complex convolutional-recurrent model is developed for benchmarking the proposed transformer model. NAS improves optimal performance of both models in capturing spatiotemporal features, while quantization, including quantization-aware training (QAT) and post-training quantization (PTQ), reduces model sizes. The KD enhances the transformer’s accuracy by transferring knowledge from the complex model, whereas the complex teacher model obtains an accuracy of 95.59% on the Widar 3.0 dataset within cross-domain (environment) settings; the distilled quantized transformer model accomplishes a similar performance with an accuracy of 93.77%, demonstrating its suitability for resource-constrained environments.
